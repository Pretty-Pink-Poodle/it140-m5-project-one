# Y2K Mall After Dark

## Game Theme and Storyline

Y2K Mall After Dark is a text-based adventure game set in Starlight Mall in 2003. The player returns to the mall shortly before closing to retrieve a bag they accidentally left behind. Before they can leave, the security gates close and the mall goes into lockdown. The player discovers that the mall's security system has malfunctioned and must travel through different areas of the mall to collect six items that will help them escape.

The player begins in the Food Court and can explore Glow Beauty, Replay Records, Pixel Palace Arcade, Flashback Photo Booth, Starstruck Department Store, and the Electronics Store. Each of these rooms contains one item that the player must collect. The six items are Lip Gloss, a Burned CD, an Arcade Token, a Photo Strip, an Employee Keycard, and a Flip Phone. The villain, known as the Night Manager, is located in the Security Office. The player wins by collecting all six items before entering the Security Office. If the player encounters the Night Manager before collecting every item, the game is over.

## Game Map

The game map is included in `game_map.drawio`. The Food Court is the starting room. The player can safely travel through the mall and collect all six items without entering the Security Office. The Security Office contains the Night Manager and should only be entered after all six items have been collected.
